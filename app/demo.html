Services › SNAP benefits › Monthly work rules (ABAWD)

Find volunteer opportunities

If you are not exempt from the ABAWD rules, not enrolled in school, and not working enough hours at a job, you can meet the monthly requirement by volunteering. The number of volunteer hours you need depends on your benefit amount; the ABAWD page on NYC.gov explains how it is calculated.

Community organizations in every borough need volunteers. Find one near you on the map below, then contact them by phone or through their website to arrange your hours. A supervisor at the organization has to sign your monthly participation form, so it helps to call before your first visit.

185 volunteer sites at 75 organizations. Select an organization to see all of its locations.
